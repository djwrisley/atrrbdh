## BRIEF DESCRIPTION OF THE WORKSHOP 

This workshop introduces automatic text recognition (ATR) as both a practical method and a critical problem for digital humanities research. Participants will gain hands-on experience with workflows for transforming digitized cultural heritage materials into machine-readable texts, including segmentation, transcription, model selection, training, and correction. We will explore how ATR-generated texts can become the basis for further digital humanities research while considering how transcription quality and modeling choices shape subsequent analysis. Moving beyond technical workflows, the workshop examines the infrastructures, platforms, biases, languages, and human labor that determine which materials become legible to machines. Ultimately, we will consider how technologies for reading the past also shape what can count as data and, therefore, the future of cultural memory.
